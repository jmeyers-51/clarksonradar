Overview

The radar at a glance. Raw scraped items land in Review — nothing goes public until you approve it.

How publishing works

  1. Make changes anywhere — Review approvals, Events, Places, Quality.
  2. Open the Publish tab and download the changed files.
  3. Send the downloaded files to Muse — they get pushed live to the radar.

The public page never shows the staging queue. Approved items appear on the map only after a push.
